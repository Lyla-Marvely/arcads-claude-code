## Audio and music direction

- **Language of ALL audible speech:** ALL audible speech is English only. No Indonesian anywhere in the audible track. Characters do not speak; there is no character dialogue.
- **Narrator:** One consistent English narrator for all six clips: warm, friendly, upbeat children's-story storyteller; clear, natural, neutral international English; moderate, unhurried pace; smiling tone; simple words. The same voice and the same voice settings for every line; never switch voices between clips.
- **Music:** Cheerful, warm, playful instrumental music for children (for example light marimba, ukulele and glockenspiel with soft pizzicato strings); gentle upbeat tempo; one continuous 30-second bed under all six clips; instrumental only, with no vocals or lyrics; a soft, warm resolving ending on the final hold.
- **Music prompt (technical, English):** Cheerful, warm, playful instrumental children's music, light marimba, ukulele and glockenspiel with soft pizzicato strings, gentle upbeat tempo, friendly and magical, no vocals, no lyrics, 30 seconds, soft warm resolving ending.
- **Ambient:** Soft forest ambience as one continuous bed under all six clips: gentle breeze, rustling leaves, a few distant birds; quiet and never harsh.
- **Sound effects:** Light, soft, kid-friendly effects synced to the visible action: footsteps on grass, leaf and flower rustle, soft wing flaps, small bird chirps and a magical shimmer or chime for the egg. Nothing loud or sharp. The egg makes only a shimmer, never a voice, heartbeat, tapping or cracking sound.
- **Mix rules:**
  - The voice-over is always the loudest element. Duck the music and ambient under every VO line so the music never covers the voice-over.
  - No empty audio: the music bed and the ambient bed run continuously across all six clips (render each as ONE 30-second track, not per clip), with VO and SFX layered on top. Cross-fade clip joins by 0.1 to 0.2 s.
  - Start each VO line about 0.25 s after its clip starts and keep it inside the clip. If a clip is longer than its VO by more than about 0.5 s, trim the clip to VO + 0.5 s instead of leaving dead air (the repo's no-dead-space rule), then re-time the music and ambient to the trimmed total.
  - The egg shimmer rises through S05 and settles gently in S06.
  - Verify by listening: no speech other than the English narrator, no stray voices, no non-English words.
- **Production path:**
  - Video clips: generate silent (Arcads Seedance `audioEnabled: false`, as in the repo's pipeline) so no stray voices or non-English speech can appear; all audio is added in post.
  - VO: one ElevenLabs voice for all lines. The repo script shared/skills/pixar-style-ad/scripts/generate-vo-elevenlabs.sh defaults to a warm American male voice ('Brian'; override with ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID). The voice choice is for the owner to approve. It needs the owner's own ElevenLabs key; this pack does not request or change any key.
  - Music: an instrumental bed. The repo script generate-music-elevenlabs.sh composes from a text prompt (same key requirement), or use a licensed track of your choice.
  - SFX: no sound-effects generator is documented in this repo. Use a licensed SFX library or the editor, following the per-clip cues. Not generated or verified here.
  - Final mix per aspect ratio with ffmpeg (see shared/skills/pixar-style-ad/scripts/final-assembly.sh for the repo's mixing approach).
